Stitch Counter is an offline row and repeat counter for knitting and crochet.

## Frequently asked questions

### How are rows and repeats counted?

Tap the large row button after finishing a row. The main count and every linked counter advance together. Manual counters only change with their own plus and minus buttons.

### Can I add pattern instructions?

Yes. Edit a project and add an instruction for an exact row or a repeating interval. Due instructions appear automatically on the counter.

### Where are my projects stored?

Projects and settings are stored locally in the app. There is no account or in-app cloud sync. Removing the app can remove its project data; a device backup may include app data depending on your operating-system settings.

### The screen does not stay awake

Open a project and enable the Awake control. It applies only while that counter session is open.

### How do I report a problem?

Email [ddaklabs@gmail.com](mailto:ddaklabs@gmail.com?subject=%5BStitch%20Counter%5D%20Support) with your device model, OS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include private pattern documents.
