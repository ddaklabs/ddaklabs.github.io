DDak Labs (“we”) operates Rider Ledger. This policy explains what stays on your device, what is sent when cloud backup is used, and what Google services may process for analytics and advertising.

<div class="callout"><strong>Summary</strong><br>Your ledger is stored on your device. The app assigns a backup ID and initializes a recoverable cloud backup; later cloud backups occur when you choose Backup. Firebase Analytics records a few feature events without amounts or notes. Google AdMob provides ads using the consent choices available in your region.</div>

## 1. Information handled

### Ledger and settings

The app stores dates, country and currency, income or expense type, platform or category, amounts, optional labels, delivery count, distance, working time, goals, tax estimates, quick amounts, appearance, locale, and backup status in a local SQLite database. Local JSON backup files may contain the same information. DDak Labs does not receive a file unless you choose to share it.

### Cloud backup

On first use, the app creates a random backup ID and sends an initial encrypted-in-transit request to the Rider Ledger backup service. Cloud backups can include the ledger and settings listed above, the backup ID, backup date, and summary totals. They do not include your name, email address, phone number, contact list, or precise location. Anyone who knows the backup ID may be able to restore that backup, so keep it private.

### Analytics and advertising

Firebase Analytics may process app launch and whether a record was created or updated or a backup was run, plus app/device information, approximate region, identifiers, and diagnostics generated by the service. The app does not put ledger amounts, platform names, custom labels, dates, distances, or working time into analytics events.

Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, ad requests, impressions, interactions, and diagnostics to provide, measure, secure, and limit ads. The app requests non-personalized ads. Where required, Google’s consent form is shown before ads are requested, and available choices can be revisited from Settings.

### Support email

If you contact us, Google’s Gmail service and DDak Labs may process your email address, message, device/app details, and attachments you choose to send.

## 2. Purposes and service providers

| Service | Provider | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rider Ledger backup API | DDak Labs on Cloudflare Workers and D1 | Backup and restore by backup ID |
| Firebase Analytics | Google LLC | Aggregate feature use and reliability |
| Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform | Google LLC | Ads, consent, measurement, and fraud prevention |
| Gmail | Google LLC | Support correspondence |

See [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Google partner-site information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). We do not sell personal information.

## 3. Retention, deletion, and choices

- Local records remain until you delete them, clear app data, or remove the app. Removing the app may permanently delete local records.
- Local backup files remain wherever you save or share them.
- A cloud backup remains available for restoration until it is overwritten or deleted by DDak Labs. To request deletion, email us with the backup ID. We may ask for information needed to verify the request.
- Google retains analytics and advertising data according to its service settings and policies.
- You can use the in-app advertising privacy option when available, change device advertising settings, delete local data, or stop using cloud backup.

## 4. Security, international processing, and children

Network requests use HTTPS. A backup ID is a recovery credential, not an account password; do not publish it. Google and Cloudflare may process information in countries other than yours. Rider Ledger is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly request a child’s name or contact details.

## 5. Contact and changes

Privacy questions, rights requests, and cloud-backup deletion requests may be sent to [ddaklabs@gmail.com](mailto:ddaklabs@gmail.com?subject=%5BRider%20Ledger%5D%20Privacy). Include your backup ID only when a backup-related request requires it; do not send unnecessary ledger details.
